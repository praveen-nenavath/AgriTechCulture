Expert Advice

Farming Tips & Best Practices

Soil Health
Test Your Soil Before Every Season

Conduct NPK and pH testing 3–4 weeks before sowing. This saves up to 30% on fertiliser costs and ensures optimal crop nutrition from day one.

Irrigation
Irrigate at the Right Crop Stage

Critical irrigation windows: germination, tillering/branching, flowering, and grain filling. Missing these reduces yield by 25–40%.

Pest Management
Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

Combine biological controls, resistant varieties, and chemical inputs as a last resort. IPM can cut pesticide costs by 50% while preserving soil biodiversity.

Post-Harvest
Reduce Post-Harvest Losses

Proper drying (to 12–14% moisture), storage in ventilated bags, and temperature control can reduce losses from 20–30% down to under 5%.

Market Timing
Time Your Market Entry

Prices typically rise 3–6 weeks after peak arrival season. If you have storage, hold back 20–30% of produce to capitalise on off-peak pricing.

Crop Rotation
Practice Smart Crop Rotation

Rotate cereals with legumes every 2nd season. Legumes fix atmospheric nitrogen (150–200 kg N/ha), reducing fertiliser costs significantly for the next crop.
